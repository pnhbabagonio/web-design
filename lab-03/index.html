What is Computer Science?

Introduction to Computer Science

Computer science is the study of computers, computational systems, and information processing. It spans both theoretical study (like algorithms, data structures, and the limits of computation) and practical application (such as designing software, hardware, and artificial intelligence).

In short, Computer science is the study of how computers work, how to solve problems using technology, and how to write instructions that tell machines what to do. It is not just about fixing hardware [0.7]; it is the creative science behind software, apps, data, and artificial intelligence.
